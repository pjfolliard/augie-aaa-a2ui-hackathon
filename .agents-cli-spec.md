# Agent Specification — Augustana University (Sioux Falls, SD) Academic Advising Agent (AAA)

## Overview
- **Agent Name:** `augie_academic_advising_a2ui_agent`
- **Institution:** Augustana University (Sioux Falls, South Dakota — `augie.edu`)
- **Framework:** Google Agent Development Kit (`google-adk`) + Google A2UI (`a2ui-agent-sdk`)
- **Purpose:** Persona-scoped Academic Advising Agent rendering WCAG 2.2 AA/AAA accessible tables, KPI cards, SOPHIA degree progress trackers, and action forms across 10 campus personas (`Admin`, `Provost`, `Registrar`, `Student Success`, `Deans`, `Area Chair`, `Program Director`, `Department Chair`, `Advisor`, `Student`).

## Data & Schema Adapter
- **Persona Matrix:** `data/personas.json` (`10 Personas × 10 Canonical Questions = 100 Views`)
- **Field Dictionary:** `data/zion_schema_mapping.json` (Hypothetical pre-Zion schema with drop-in mapping for the official **Zion Cloud Solutions** field dictionary)

## Safety, RBAC & Accessibility Guardrails
- **Strict Advising Scope Enforcement:** `All (University-Wide)` → `All (Systems & Compliance)` → `All in School/College` → `All in Area (Arts & Sciences)` → `All in Program` → `All in Department` → `Assigned Advisees Only` → `Self Only`.
- **Universal Accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA/AAA):** Mandatory table captions, row/column scopes, non-color status badges (`✓`, `⚠`, `⛔`, `🌐`), 100% visual/text parity for deaf users, plain-language US higher-ed glossary, and F-1 international student 12-credit full-time guardrails.
